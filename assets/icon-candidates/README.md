# App icon candidates

These three concepts were generated with the built-in image generation tool for
the independent `xiaomi-lightbar-mac` project. They do not contain the Xiaomi
logo; the project name describes device compatibility, not affiliation.

- `01-warm-ivory.png`: light ivory and warm amber, straight-on monitor.
- `02-dark-copper.png`: dark graphite and copper, three-dimensional monitor.
- `03-bright-orange.png`: bright orange gradient, simplified monitor and beam.

The user selected candidate 1. Its cleaned, square version is saved as
`../app-icon.png` and packaged for macOS as `../AppIcon.icns`.

## Generation prompts

### 1. Warm ivory

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single original macOS application icon on a truly transparent canvas. Design ONE polished, square rounded icon: a minimalist friendly flat illustration of a monitor with a distinct short cylindrical screen light bar resting horizontally on its top edge, clearly casting a warm amber glow down onto the monitor. Warm pale ivory and charcoal with a restrained tangerine accent; sophisticated, simple, instantly recognizable at small Dock size. Symmetrical straight-on view, very clean geometry, premium native Mac app aesthetics. Make the entire icon with comfortable transparent margins around the rounded square. No words, letters, numbers, logos, trademarks, symbols resembling the Xiaomi/MI logo, watermark, or people. Original design for a third-party controller compatible with the Xiaomi first-generation monitor light bar, without suggesting manufacturer endorsement.

Cleanup edit: Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied macOS app icon selected by the user. Preserve the existing ivory rounded-square tile, front-facing monitor, horizontal black screen light bar, screen pattern, colors, composition and lighting as closely as possible. Remove ONLY the scattered loose white and colored pixels and smudges outside the rounded-square tile. Make the area beyond the tile clean, completely transparent, with a crisp but naturally antialiased tile edge. Center the full tile on a square transparent canvas with modest even margins. No letters, no words, no logos, no trademarks, no extra objects.

### 2. Dark copper

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single original macOS application icon on a truly transparent canvas. Design ONE distinctive rounded-square icon: premium dimensional 3D illustration on a deep midnight graphite tile, a sleek horizontal graphite cylindrical monitor light bar perched across the top of a simple widescreen monitor, warm light illuminating the upper screen and a gentle halo, rich copper/amber highlights. Refined macOS app icon, recognizable when small, restrained and uncluttered. Three-quarter perspective but the monitor and horizontal mounted light bar must both be clearly recognizable. Comfortable transparent margins around rounded square. No words, letters, numbers, logos, trademarks, symbols resembling the Xiaomi/MI logo, watermark, or people. Original design for a third-party controller compatible with the Xiaomi first-generation monitor light bar, no endorsement implied.

### 3. Bright orange

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single original macOS application icon on a truly transparent canvas. Design ONE bold flat geometric rounded-square icon: an original front-facing monitor outline with a thick short horizontal black light bar perched directly atop its upper edge, emitting an elegant warm beam in the shape of a soft arc across the screen. Background is a tasteful warm persimmon-to-honey gradient, screen is ivory and dark slate, bright modern welcoming visual character, native macOS style. Very simple strong silhouette for small Dock sizes, centered with comfortable transparent margins around rounded square. Avoid desk lamps, bulbs, wires, tiny details, words, letters, numbers, logos, trademarks, symbols resembling the Xiaomi/MI logo, watermark, or people. Original design for a third-party controller compatible with the Xiaomi first-generation monitor light bar, without suggesting manufacturer endorsement.
